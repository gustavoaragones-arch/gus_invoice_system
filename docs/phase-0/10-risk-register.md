# 10 — Risk Register

Status: Draft for Project Director review
Phase: 0

Severity and likelihood are qualitative (High / Medium / Low). No numerical probabilities are assigned — Phase 0 has no empirical basis (usage data, incident history) to support quantified figures, and fabricating numbers would misrepresent confidence. Qualitative ratings reflect: severity = impact if the risk materializes on financial/compliance/security grounds; likelihood = a reasoned judgment based on the nature of the mechanism involved (e.g., manual data entry is inherently more error-prone than a deterministic calculation), not on historical frequency data.

## Notes on Reasoning

- Likelihood ratings for Calendar-related risks (008–011) are rated Medium because heuristic matching against real-world calendar data is inherently imperfect regardless of implementation quality — this is a property of the problem, not a prediction from data.
- Likelihood for RISK-012 (OAuth exposure) and RISK-018 (authorization failure) is rated Low specifically because the governing requirements (06-security-requirements.md) mandate defense-in-depth (server-side + database-level enforcement); the risk is rated High severity because, if the mitigation is not correctly implemented, the consequence is severe.
- RISK-016/017 (migration/historical data) cannot yet be rated with confidence beyond "Medium/Medium" because Phase 0 has no information about Business A's actual historical data format or quality — this is itself recorded as an unresolved decision, not resolved by assumption.
